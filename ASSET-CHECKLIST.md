# Optional asset replacement checklist

The portfolio is complete and understandable without replacement images. Each frame contains an honest label rather than a fake screenshot. When suitable originals are available, add optimized WebP files under `public/images/` and update the corresponding archive entry.

## Requested images

1. **CodeClash platform screenshot** — `public/images/codeclash-platform.webp`
   - Preferred size: 1600 × 1000 pixels
   - Use a clean product screen with no account email, token, private team data, unpublished event information, or browser chrome.

2. **Research poster** — `public/images/research-poster.webp`
   - Preferred size: 1600–2000 pixels wide
   - Export the original poster or provide a readable representative crop. Confirm all co-author and institutional marks may be shown publicly.

3. **Firmware development bench** — `public/images/firmware-bench.webp`
   - Preferred size: 1600 × 1000 pixels
   - Show the STM32/CAN setup without serial numbers, private notes, credentials, or proprietary diagrams.

4. **Teaching materials** — `public/images/teaching-materials.webp`
   - Preferred size: 1600 × 1000 pixels
   - Use Maria’s original explanatory material only. Remove course-owned assessment questions, student names, grades, messages, and identifiable feedback.

5. **Professional portrait** — `public/images/portrait.webp` (optional)
   - Preferred size: 1200 × 1500 pixels, 4:5 crop
   - Neutral or simple background; the About page works without it.

6. **Robotics artifact** — `public/images/robotics-work.webp` (optional)
   - Preferred size: 1600 × 1000 pixels
   - Prefer the robot or competition artifact alone. Do not publish identifiable minors without explicit consent.
